scripts/build-dictionary.mjs from:

- ENABLE word list (public domain).
- FrequencyWords by Hermit Dave (https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords), content

- A blocklist from LDNOOBW (https://github.com/LDNOOBW/List-of-Dirty-Naughty-Obscene-and-Otherwise-Bad-Words),
  CC BY 4.0, used to remove offensive words.
