Privacy policy

DPIYF Lettertown · Last updated 29 September 2026

DPIYF Lettertown is a daily word game. This page explains what the game stores, why, who handles it, and how to delete it. There are no ads, no tracking across other apps or websites, and nothing is sold.

What we collect

  • Your leaderboard name, if you choose one. It is shown to everyone on the leaderboard.
  • A random player ID created on your device, so your scores and progress stay together. It isn't linked to your phone number, email or any other account.
  • Your game activity: the words you find, your scores, and which days you played.
  • Rooms you join and room chat messages you send. Messages are shown with your leaderboard name to the members of that room only. Offensive words are masked automatically.
  • Approximate location (city, region and country), worked out by our server host from your connection when you open the game. It is used only to let the game's owner know roughly where people are playing from. We do not store your IP address or your precise location.

Your device also keeps your progress and settings in its own storage so the game works offline.

How it's used

  • To run the daily and Blitz leaderboards and check scores.
  • To let you continue your game on another device.
  • To send the game's owner short notifications about activity (for example "3 new players from New York") so they can see how the game is doing and look after it.

Who handles it

  • Cloudflare runs the leaderboard server and stores the data above.
  • GitHub hosts the game. Owner notifications are relayed through GitHub Actions, whose logs keep the notification text (names, rough locations, scores) for up to 90 days.
  • ntfy.sh delivers those notifications to the owner's phone.

Each handles the data only to provide their service to us.

Keeping and deleting your data

Data is kept while the game runs. You can delete it at any time: open Leaderboard and tap delete my data. That immediately removes your name, scores, found words, visit history, room memberships and chat messages from our server and clears the game from your device. You can also delete any of your chat messages yourself, and a room's host can delete the room and its chat. Each room keeps only its latest 150 messages. Copies inside notification logs expire within 90 days.

Reporting names and messages

Tap any name on the leaderboard, or any chat message, to report it or hide that player. Reports are reviewed and offensive names or messages are renamed or removed.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has added a name, contact us and we'll remove it.

Contact

Questions or requests: open an issue on GitHub.
